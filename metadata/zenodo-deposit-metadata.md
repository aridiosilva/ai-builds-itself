## Description / Abstract

This independent bilingual report provides an academic and didactic analysis
of the Anthropic Institute article “When AI Builds Itself: Our Progress Toward
Recursive Self-Improvement, and Its Implications.” It distinguishes
AI-accelerated research and development from full recursive self-improvement,
examines the empirical evidence presented by Anthropic, identifies
methodological limitations and unresolved logical steps, and discusses
implications for scientific practice, organizational design, human expertise,
AI safety, and governance. The analysis concludes that current evidence
strongly supports the claim that AI already accelerates important parts of AI
development, but does not yet demonstrate that present systems can
autonomously select consequential research goals, validate results, design
superior successors, or safely close the entire improvement loop. Complete
English and Portuguese editions are included in this record.
